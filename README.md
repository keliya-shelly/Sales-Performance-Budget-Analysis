# Sales-Performance-Budget-Analysis

📊 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end Sales Analytics Dashboard built using
SQL, Python/Pandas, data modelling, DAX, and Power BI.

The objective is to transform raw sales, customer, product, and calendar
data into an interactive business intelligence solution that helps users
understand:

Overall sales performance

Sales vs. budget

Top customers

Top products

Product category contribution

Monthly sales trends

Customer-level sales performance

Product-level sales performance

Geographic sales distribution

The dashboard is designed from a business-user perspective, with
interactive filters for year, month, customer city, sub-category,
product category, and product name.

🎯 Business Objective

The main goal of this project is to answer practical business questions
such as:

How are actual sales performing against the budget?

Which customers contribute the most to total sales?

Which products generate the highest sales?

Which product categories dominate the portfolio?

How does sales performance change month by month?

Which customers and products are driving overall revenue?

Where are sales geographically concentrated?

💡 Business Insights Supported by the Dashboard
The dashboard provides a clear overview of sales performance and business growth by comparing actual sales with the budget. It helps identify the top-performing customers and products, understand which product categories contribute the most to overall sales, and track how sales change across different months. The dashboard also provides a geographical view of sales, helping identify cities with higher sales contributions. With interactive filters for year, month, customer city, sub-category, product category, and product name, users can explore specific areas of the business and understand how customer and product performance changes based on different selections. Overall, the dashboard helps convert raw sales data into meaningful business insights that can support better decision-making and performance monitoring.

The purpose is to move from raw transactional data → meaningful
business information → actionable analysis.
